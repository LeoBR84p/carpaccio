Privacy

We don't know who you are. By design.

    No user identification

    Your Telegram ID is only used to route the download link back to you. It is never persisted to any database or log file.

    Ephemeral processing

    Every download job runs in an isolated container that is destroyed immediately after the file is transferred to the delivery node.

    Files are served from Iceland — one of the strongest privacy jurisdictions in the world, with no data retention laws.

    Privacy-preserving payments

    Telegram Stars contain no personally identifiable billing data. Revenue is converted to Monero — a privacy coin with ring signatures and stealth addresses.

FAQ

Common questions.

What platforms are supported? +
Anything supported by yt-dlp — which covers YouTube, Vimeo, Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, Twitch (VODs and clips), SoundCloud, Dailymotion, and over a thousand others. If yt-dlp can handle it, so can we. Embed-based players (JWPlayer, DoodStream, Redgifs, etc.) are also handled through our custom extractor.
How long does processing take? +
Most links are ready in 10–45 seconds. Longer videos or rare high-bitrate formats may take up to 2 minutes. You'll receive a Telegram notification the moment your link is ready — no need to wait in the chat.
Can I share the download link with someone else? +
Technically yes, but it only works once. The first device that opens the link downloads the file; immediately after, the token is invalidated and the file is deleted. There's no way to regenerate the same link.
What happens to my Stars if the download fails? +
If the bot cannot extract a working download URL from your link within the processing window, the Stars charge is cancelled before it processes. You are never charged for a failed job. The Stars invoice is only finalized after a valid link is generated.
Is there a file size limit? +
Standard plan: up to 500 MB per download. Full Quality plan: up to 4 GB. Files larger than 4 GB are not supported at this time. Audio-only downloads have no practical size limit.
Do you log what URLs I send? +
No. The URL is passed directly to the extraction job and discarded after the job completes. There is no database record of URL submissions, download requests, or user-to-link associations. The only transient state is the job queue entry, which is purged after completion whether successful or not.
